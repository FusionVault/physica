# Gravity DIRDs (AAWSAP, 2010–2011) and the physics that has tested or extended them

Scope note: only public material was used. The core DIRD PDFs were read from The Black Vault's copies of the DIA FOIA release; the OCR text layer was supplemented by reading equation pages as images. Numbers marked **[checked]** were recomputed with `dirds_checks.py` (same folder; pure Python, `python3 -I dirds_checks.py`).

Labels used throughout: **[GR]** = mainstream general relativity or QFT, peer reviewed and accepted; **[SPEC]** = peer-reviewed or mathematically valid but physically speculative or disputed; **[FRINGE]** = unreplicated, contradicted or outside mainstream physics.

## Q2. Key equations and numbers in each gravity DIRD (checked where possible)

### Takeaway
The DIRDs mostly restate textbook GR and quantum field theory formulas correctly: the Schwarzschild and Reissner–Nordström metrics, Alcubierre, Morris–Thorne, Casimir, and the quadrupole formula. Their headline numbers reproduce to 3 significant figures. I found several internal arithmetic or dimensional errors, though: DIRD 13 (eq. 2.4 inversion; Table 3 versus its stated volume), DIRD 21 (eq. 2 as printed; the Shannon capacity uses ln instead of log₂), and a 13% discrepancy in DIRD 18 Table 1. The speculative parts are interpretations, not derivations: Puthoff's craft-signature table, Obousy's extra-dimension warp, Davis's "stargate", and Baker's HFGW link budget. Puthoff's PV formula K = exp(2GM/rc²) is **not** in DIRD 15. It is in his 2002 *Foundations of Physics* paper (see Q3).

### Cited Findings

#### DIRD 15 — Puthoff, "Advanced Space Propulsion Based on Vacuum (Spacetime Metric) Engineering" (29 Mar 2010) [GR maths, SPEC interpretation]
Source: [DIRD 15 PDF](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dia/AAWSAP-DIRDs/DIRD_15-DIRD_Advanced_Space_Propulsion_Based_on_Vacuum_Spacetime_Metric_Engineer.pdf), pp.1–11.
- (1) ds² = g_μν dx^μ dx^ν. (2) Minkowski: ds² = c²dt² − (dx²+dy²+dz²), signature (+,−,−,−).
- (4) Mass m at the origin: ds² = [(1−Gm/rc²)/(1+Gm/rc²)] c²dt² − [(1−Gm/rc²)/(1+Gm/rc²)]⁻¹ dr² − (1+Gm/rc²)² r²(dθ²+sin²θ dφ²). **[checked]** This is the Schwarzschild metric in harmonic radial coordinate r = R − Gm/c², where R is the areal radius. g₀₀ equals 1−2Gm/(Rc²) to machine precision.
- (5) Reissner–Nordström in the same coordinates: add the term Q²G/(4πε₀c⁴) / [r²(1+Gm/rc²)²] to g₀₀ and g₁₁⁻¹. The charge term has the opposite sign to the mass term, which the DIRD calls "electrogravitic repulsion".
- Proper intervals are √(−g_ii) dx^i and √g₀₀ dt.
- (6)–(8) Coordinate light speed v_L = c √(g₀₀/(−g₁₁)). Effective index n = √(−g₁₁/g₀₀).
- Table 1, as seen by a remote observer:
  - Δt → Δt/√g₀₀
  - ω → ω√g₀₀
  - E → E√g₀₀
  - δr → δr/√(−g₁₁)
  - v_L = c√(g₀₀/−g₁₁)
  - m → (−g₁₁/√g₀₀) m
- "Engineered" craft would have g₀₀ > 1 and |g₁₁| < 1. The DIRD lists the effects this would cause: blueshifted infrared appearing as visible glow, "hardened" materials, superluminal coordinate speed, and reduced effective mass.
- The only propulsion metric discussed is Alcubierre's, and the DIRD concedes that "the required energy densities predicted by present theory exceed by many orders of magnitude values achievable" (p. iv).

## Q5. Which calculations make sound, verifiable Rust code, and which do not

### Takeaway
Implement the mainstream GR/QFT layer, where published closed-form results and numbers exist to test against. Treat the DIRD-specific engineering claims only as regression tests that reproduce the *documents* (including their errors), not physics.

### Cited Findings: good Rust targets with published validation values
1. **Energy conditions for a metric** (the Warp Factory approach; [repo](https://github.com/NerdsWithAttitudes/WarpFactory), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03095)). Steps:
   - metric g_μν(x) on a grid;
   - 4th-order finite differences, or forward-mode dual numbers;
   - Christoffel symbols, then Riemann, Ricci and Einstein tensors, then T_μν = (c⁴/8πG)G_μν;
   - contract with sampled null and timelike observers in an orthonormal tetrad (NEC, WEC, DEC, SEC).

   Validation targets:
   - Schwarzschild gives T = 0.
   - Alcubierre Eulerian energy density gives −(c⁴/32πG)v²(y²+z²)/r_s²·f′² ([Pfenning–Ford eq 8](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9702026)).
   - Morris–Thorne at the throat gives ρ + p_r < 0, with τ₀ = c⁴/(8πG b₀²) = 4.8×10⁴⁰ Pa at 10 m.
   - Fuchs shell (R₁ = 10, R₂ = 20 m, M = 4.49×10²⁷ kg, β = 0.02): no violations ([arXiv:2405.02709](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02709)).
   - SSV's theorem that NEC is violated for generic superluminal drives ([arXiv:2105.03079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03079)).
2. **Total and ADM energy.**
   - E = −(v²/12)∫r²f′²dr reproduces Pfenning–Ford: about 6.9×10⁶² v kg for R = 100 m and Δ = 100 v L_P (paper: 6.2×10⁶²), and 0.56 M_⊙ for Δ = 1 m.
   - The ADM mass from the 1/r fall-off of g_rr in an asymptotically flat metric. The Fuchs shell must return 4.49×10²⁷ kg; the subtleties are in [Schuster–Santiago–Visser 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.15950).
3. **Quantum-inequality bounds** (closed form): ρ̂_min = −3ħc/(32π²(cτ₀)⁴). This gives −3.7×10⁻²⁶ J/m³ for τ₀ = 1 ns and −3.7×10⁻² J/m³ for τ₀ = 1 fs **[checked]**. Use it as a guard on any negative-energy input ([Pfenning–Ford eq 9](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9702026)).
4. **Casimir.**
   - Ideal plates: u = −π²ħc/(720d⁴) and P = −π²ħc/(240d⁴), giving −1.30 mPa at 1 µm.
   - Sphere–plate PFA: −π³ħcR/(360d³).
   - Weight: |E|g/c² ([Fulling et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0702091)).
   - The real-metal Lifshitz corrections are a larger, separate job.
5. **PV/exponential metric versus Schwarzschild light bending and orbits.**
   - Null-geodesic quadrature in isotropic coordinates with index n = √(B/A).
   - Validate 1.7512″ (solar limb), 42.98″/century (Mercury), and the 2nd-order coefficients 4π and 15π/4.
   - The exponential-metric throat is at r = GM/c² with areal radius e·GM/c² ([Puthoff 2002](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9909037); [Boonserm et al. 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.03781)).
   - This fits the project's geodesy stack: it is a 1PN-equivalent alternative metric whose differences appear only at 2PN.
6. **Thin-shell and wormhole bookkeeping.**
   - The Lanczos surface stress from the principal curvatures (DIRD 18 eqs 2–3).
   - Visser cube edges at −c⁴/(8G) = −1.513×10⁴³ J/m ([arXiv:0809.0907](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.0907)).
   - The VKD volume integral, eq 13 ([arXiv:gr-qc/0301003](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0301003)).
   - MTY Casimir plate spacing d = 4.05×10⁻¹⁸ √r m, giving 1.28×10⁻¹⁶ m at 1 km.
7. **GW flux and strain conversions.**
   - P = (32G/5c⁵)(2rΔf/Δt)² = 1.764×10⁻⁵²(…)².
   - A = k√F/ν, with k = √(2G/πc³) = 1.256×10⁻¹⁸ under the F = c³ω²A²/(8πG) convention.
   - Compare against measured limits: h_c ≲ 6×10⁻²⁶ near 10¹⁵ Hz ([Ejlli et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.00232)).

### Cited Findings: do not implement as physics (reproduce only as document checks)
- **Obousy's extra-dimension warp energies (DIRD 13 §7).** These rest only on proportionalities (ρ ~ 1/R⁴, H ~ 1/R²) with no metric. Table 3 is internally inconsistent: the 100 m³ volume gives 10⁴⁴ J, not 10⁴² J, and the v = 100 row breaks v² scaling. — [DIRD 13](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dia/AAWSAP-DIRDs/DIRD_13-DIRD_Warp_Drive_Dark_energy_and_the_Manipulation_of_Extra_Dimensions.pdf)
- **Gravitationally squeezed vacuum ρ = −2π²ħc/λ⁴ (DIRD 18).** Davis himself reports Ford's objection, which shifts the result by about 50 orders of magnitude. — [DIRD 18](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dia/AAWSAP-DIRDs/DIRD_18-DIRD_Traversible_Wormholes_Stargates_and_Negative_Energy.pdf)
- **HFGW link budget (DIRD 21).**
  - The arithmetic is implementable: the Shannon capacity is 2.82×10⁶ bps, not 1.9×10⁶.
  - The physical inputs have no empirical support. These are the 10¹⁴ W/m² generator, the N² coherence gain and the A ~ 10⁻³² detector, and JASON (2008) judged that no practical use of HFGW is credible.
  - Sources: [DIRD 21](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dia/AAWSAP-DIRDs/DIRD_21-DIRD_High-Frequency_Gravitational_Wave_Communications.pdf); [FAS](https://fas.org/?p=10244)
- **Podkletnov and Tajmar effects, ZPE extraction devices, Pinto lift, Winterberg's Planck aether (DIRDs 14, 19, 24, 29).** Either there is no quantitative model, or the effects are null in replication (Hathaway's own replication; NASA).
- **Puthoff DIRD 15 Table 1 "craft signatures".** These are correct metric identities, but they become predictions only once someone supplies a source for the metric. Implementing them adds nothing beyond item 5 above.
- **Lentz soliton.** The metric could be checked, but the result is the SSV dispute over observer dependence, and his energy estimate contains an unspecified form factor C ~ O(1). — [arXiv:2006.07125](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07125); [arXiv:2105.03079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03079)

### Inferences
- The highest-value, lowest-risk Rust module is a generic "metric → stress-energy → energy-condition minima" evaluator, validated on Schwarzschild, Alcubierre, Morris–Thorne and the Fuchs shell. Everything in item 2, and the VKD integrals, then fall out as quadratures over its output.
- For this repository, which works on satellite gravimetry, only the PV versus GR comparison and the Casimir/QI closed forms touch measurable gravity. The rest is GR exotica with no observational data to validate against beyond published numbers.

## Which missions have open data, and what is the 2026 status of each?

### Takeaway
Anyone can download open data today from GRACE (2002–2017), GRACE-FO (2018–present, still producing monthly fields; August 2026 is the latest month at PO.DAAC), GOCE (2009–2013, ESA; free but needs a login), CHAMP (as derived models on ICGEM), Swarm (monthly GPS-derived gravity fields to degree 40, anonymous) and SLR low-degree series. No Chinese GRACE-like mission data and no in-orbit quantum or cold-atom gravity data has been publicly released.

**Swarm (ESA, 2013–present): gravity from GPS / kinematic orbits**
- Product `EGF_SHA_2_` is the monthly Earth gravity field from multi-approach Swarm GPS solutions (AIUB, ASU, IfG/TUG, OSU, combined). The anonymous listing on 2026-10-22 shows 145 monthly files from `SW_OPER_EGF_SHA_2__20131201T000000_20131231T235959_0101.ZIP` to `..._20251201T000000_20251231T235959_0101.ZIP`. The latest batch was posted 2026-04-24, so the product lags by several months and is released quarterly — [swarm-diss listing API](https://swarm-diss.eo.esa.int/?do=list&maxfiles=500&pos=0&file=swarm%2FLevel2longterm%2FEGF)
- The models are "published quarterly, pending the successful processing of kinematic orbits" under high solar activity, and are available from ESA's Swarm server and ICGEM — [EGU25-11903](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU25/EGU25-11903.html)
- They agree with GRACE to about 4 cm EWH over land at 750 km smoothing, and were designed to bridge the GRACE/GRACE-FO gap — [ESSD 2020, Teixeira da Encarnação et al.](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/12/1385/2020/)
- ICGEM also hosts "COST-G Swarm" (40x40 plus GAX products) and "IGG-Swarm" (Bonn) series — [ICGEM temporal list](https://icgem.gfz.de/sl/temporal); [COST-G Swarm series](https://icgem.gfz.de/sp/02_COST-G_/Swarm)

**Quantum / cold-atom sensors**
- No in-orbit cold-atom gravimetry data has been publicly released. The closest result is China Space Station atom-interferometer gyroscope/accelerometer results (acceleration resolution about 1 µm/s² single shot), which are not gravity-field products — [National Science Review 2025](https://academic.oup.com/nsr/article/12/4/nwaf012/7951977)

- JPL's quantum gravity gradiometer pathfinder is in development — [The Quantum Insider, 2025-04-16](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/04/16/nasa-to-launch-first-space-based-quantum-gravity-sensor-to-map-earths-hidden-shifts/)

### Inferences
- GRACE-FO data flow is healthy in October 2026. Level-2 lags about 1–2 months (August 2026 published around 1–2 October 2026) and L1B lags about 2 weeks.
- GRACE RL07 at PO.DAAC (CSR, JPL) means the planned RL07 reprocessing has begun to appear for the GRACE period only. GRACE-FO RL07 was planned for "Spring '26" but is not yet in CMR, so it has slipped. The empty `GRACEFO_L1A_..._RL05` collection fits GFZ's statement that RL07 waits on "reprocessed V05 Level-1B products".
- An app should treat RL06.3 (GRACE-FO) and RL06/RL07 (GRACE) as the current choices. It should query CMR dynamically rather than hard-code release numbers, because new `_0070` or `_0700` GRACE-FO collections are expected.

## Product levels: what exists, who makes it, and the exact product identifiers

### Takeaway
GRACE-FO products come in a clean hierarchy. L1A/L1B instrument data come as daily tarballs. L2 is monthly spherical harmonics (GSM plus GAA/GAB/GAC/GAD background averages) from CSR, JPL and GFZ, with combinations by COST-G and alternatives from ITSG and Tongji. L3 is gridded TWS and OBP (spherical-harmonic-derived 1° grids plus JPL, CSR and GSFC mascons). L4 is basin and ice time series. Auxiliary inputs are TN-13 (degree 1), TN-14 (C20/C30), GIA grids and AOD1B. Static or combined models are best fetched from ICGEM.

### Cited Findings
**Level-1 (GRACE-FO)**
- The L1B product IDs in the GRACE-FO L1 Handbook include KBR1B, LRI1B, ACC1B, ACT1B (transplant/hybrid accelerometer), SCA1B (star camera), GNV1B (GNSS navigation), CLK1B, GPS1B, MAS1B, THR1B, TIM1B, VKB1B/VGN1B/VSL1B (vector offsets), AHK1B/IHK1B/LHK1B (housekeeping) and others. File naming is `PRDID_YYYY-MM-DD_S_VV.txt`, where S = C, D, or Y (combined C+D) and VV = software version (0 = quicklook). Each file has a YAML header ending at `# End of YAML header`, followed by time-tagged records — [GRACE-FO L1 Handbook, JPL D-56935](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L1_Handbook.pdf)
- PO.DAAC distributes L1B as daily `gracefo_1B_YYYY-MM-DD_RL04.ascii.LRI.tgz` and `.noLRI.tgz` bundles (`GRACEFO_L1B_ASCII_GRAV_JPL_RL04`). L1A is in `GRACEFO_L1A_ASCII_GRAV_JPL_RL04` — [CMR](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GRACEFO_L1B_ASCII_GRAV_JPL_RL04&provider=POCLOUD&sort_key=-start_date&page_size=2)
- GRACE-FO SDS deliveries: L-1 weekly bundles, L-2 plus L1B-ACX monthly, and L-3/L-4 monthly — [Landerer 2025](https://cpaess.ucar.edu/sites/default/files/grace-fo-2025-presentation/landerer-felix.pdf)

**Level-2 spherical harmonics (SDS centres)**
- There are generally four files per month per centre. CSR provides `GAC-2_…_BC01_0603` and `GAD-2_…_BC01_0603` (degree 180) and `GSM-2_…_BA01_0603` (d/o 60) and `GSM-2_…_BB01_0603` (d/o 96). GSM fields are unconstrained, contain only formal sigmas, and "a suitable truncation or smoothing technique is recommended" — [CSR RL06.3 release notes](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2-ReleaseNotes_CSR_RL06.3.txt)
- The PID table covers GSM, GCM, GAA (GFZ and JPL only), GAB (GFZ and JPL only), GAC (all centres) and GAD (all centres), all in format GRCOF2. Calibrated errors, if provided, are in a companion `GSM-2_…​.txt` file — [GRACE-FO L2 User Handbook v1.1](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2_UserHandbook.pdf)
- GFZ GRACE-FO RL06.3 status (Oct 2025): 84 monthly solutions from June 2018 to July 2025, and 247 consistent GRACE+GRACE-FO monthly solutions in total. RL06/RL06.1/RL06.3 normal equations are in SINEX at ISDC, e.g. `https://isdc-data.gfz.de/grace-fo/Level-2/GFZ/RL06.3_NEQs_SINEX/` — [Dahle 2025](https://cpaess.ucar.edu/sites/default/files/grace-fo-2025-presentation/dahle-christoph-sds.pdf); [GFZ RL06.3 release notes (search result)](https://isdcftp-ng.gfz.de/grace-fo/DOCUMENTS/RELEASE_NOTES/GRACE-FO_GFZ_L2_Release_Notes_for_RL06.3.pdf)
- CSR also offers RL06.1 LRI-only Level-2 solutions for GRACE-FO, RL06.3 "Total Variation" solutions, RL06.2 Dynamic Ocean mascons, GGM05C and GIF63 — [CSR GRACE portal](https://www2.csr.utexas.edu/grace/)

## Access points: how a program fetches the data (anonymous vs. account)

### Takeaway
There are three access tiers. (1) Anonymous HTTPS: ICGEM (all .gfc models and series), TU Graz ITSG, CSR mascons, GSFC mascons, the Swarm EGF products, the PO.DAAC *docs* bucket and *public* sidecars, and (historically) GFZ ISDC, which has been HTTPS-only since 2025. (2) A free NASA Earthdata Login is required for all PO.DAAC data payloads (L1/L2/L3 files), by HTTPS or by S3 in us-west-2. (3) A free ESA EO Sign In is required for GOCE downloads. Discovery for NASA is through the CMR REST API, which needs no auth.

## File formats in detail

### Takeaway
There are three text formats and two NetCDF families to parse. (1) ICGEM `.gfc` is whitespace-separated with a header ending in `end_of_head` and keys gfc/gfct/trnd/dot/asin/acos; icgem1.0 and icgem2.0 differ in the time columns. (2) GRACE Level-2 SDS files have a YAML header and `GRCOF2` records. (3) L1B files have a YAML header and fixed per-product columns. NetCDF covers the JPL mascons (`lwe_thickness`, `uncertainty`, `land_mask`, `scale_factor`, `mascon_ID`, `GAD`, time in days since 2002-01-01) and the Tellus 1° grids.

### Cited Findings
**ICGEM .gfc (spec dated 16 Feb 2023, Förste, Barthelmes, Ince)**
- A file has three sections: an optional comment section ending at `begin_of_head`, a header ending at `end_of_head`, then data. Records are free-format, separated by blanks or tabs. Unknown keywords are comments. E-notation is recommended — [ICGEM format PDF](https://icgem.gfz.de/files/6defd9f87a64b16ec96bd657a74d0292ae3447cc7418da5104441102d8cb2b58.pdf)
- Mandatory header keys: `product_type` ("gravity_field"), `modelname`, `earth_gravity_constant` [m³s⁻²], `radius` [m], `max_degree`, `errors` ("no" | "calibrated" | "formal" | "calibrated_and_formal"), and `end_of_head`. `format` ("icgem1.0" | "icgem2.0") is mandatory if gfct/trnd/asin/acos are given piecewise per period. Optional: `tide_system` ("zero_tide" | "tide_free" | "mean_tide" | "unknown", default unknown) and `norm` ("fully_normalized" default | "unnormalized") — [ICGEM format PDF](https://icgem.gfz.de/files/6defd9f87a64b16ec96bd657a74d0292ae3447cc7418da5104441102d8cb2b58.pdf)
- icgem1.0 data columns: `gfc L M C S [sigC sigS | sigC_cal sigS_cal sigC_formal sigS_formal]`, with 4/6/8 values depending on `errors`. `gfct` adds `t0` (yyyymmdd). `trnd` has the same columns as gfc (no time). `dot` is the legacy synonym of trnd and must not be mixed with asin/acos. `asin` and `acos` append `period[year]`. trnd/asin/acos require a matching gfct — [ICGEM format PDF](https://icgem.gfz.de/files/6defd9f87a64b16ec96bd657a74d0292ae3447cc7418da5104441102d8cb2b58.pdf)
- icgem2.0: `gfct`, `trnd`, `asin` and `acos` all carry `t0 t1` in `yyyymmdd.hhmm` (validity interval), and asin/acos then add the period. Column counts are gfct 6/8/10, trnd 6/8/10 and asin/acos 7/9/11. Mixing 1.0 and 2.0 gfct is not allowed. The evaluation formula is `G(t)=G(t0)+trnd*(t-t0)+asin1*sin(2π/p1*(t-t0))+acos1*cos(2π/p1*(t-t0))+…` with t in years (e.g. p1 = 1.0 y, p2 = 0.5 y). Example lines: `gfct 2 0 -4.84165299806e-04 0.0e+00 1.9482e-13 0.0e+00 20050101` (1.0) and `gfct 1 0 7.01776593302E-11 0.0E+00 1.3180E-13 0.0E+00 19500101.0000 20020815.0817` (2.0) — [ICGEM format PDF](https://icgem.gfz.de/files/6defd9f87a64b16ec96bd657a74d0292ae3447cc7418da5104441102d8cb2b58.pdf)
- Real-world deviations a parser must tolerate. GOCO06s puts `modelname` before `product_type`, has a free-text column-legend line before `end_of_head`, and uses gfct/trnd/acos/asin with `t0 [yyyymmdd]` — [GOCO06s.gfc](https://icgem.gfz.de/getmodel/gfc/32ec2884630a02670476f752d2a2bf1c395d8c8d6d768090ed95b4f04b0d5863/GOCO06s.gfc). COST-G monthly files have no `begin_of_head`, begin with `CMMNT` lines, use non-spec keys (`product_name`, `generating_institute`, `time_period 2026182-2026212`) and use `errors empirical`, which is not in the spec's enumerated values — [COST-G Jul 2026 .gfc](https://icgem.gfz.de/getseries/02_COST-G_/Grace-Grace-FO_RL02.1/GSM-2_2026182-2026212_GRFO_COSTG_BF01_0201.gfc). Swarm EGF files use `comment` lines, `time_period_of_data 20251201-20251231`, `errors no`, `max_degree 40`, and a stray `WARNING: No quality found for 2025-12` line in the header. Inside the ZIP there is a `.gfc` plus an XML `.HDR` — [Swarm EGF Dec 2025 ZIP](https://swarm-diss.eo.esa.int/?do=download&file=swarm%2FLevel2longterm%2FEGF%2FSW_OPER_EGF_SHA_2__20251201T000000_20251231T235959_0101.ZIP)

**GRACE/GRACE-FO Level-2 (GSM/GAx) files**
- Name pattern: `PID-2_YYYYDOY-YYYYDOY_dddd_sssss_mmmm_rrvv`. PID is G + {S, C, A} + {M, U, A, B, C, D}. dddd is GRAC or GRFO; sssss is UTCSR, JPLEM or GFZOP. In mmmm, the 1st char is the observation type (A/B/C = MWI range/range-rate/range-acceleration; D/E/F = LRI; G/H/I = MWI+LRI); the 2nd char is the expansion (A = 60×60, B = 96×96, C = 180×180, D = 60×30, E = 100×100, F = 90×90); 01 = unconstrained, boxcar window. rrvv is release plus version, e.g. 0603 — [CSR RL06.3 notes](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2-ReleaseNotes_CSR_RL06.3.txt); [L2 Handbook](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2_UserHandbook.pdf)
- "Level-2 products starting from and higher than Release-06 are released with the new header. The new header is written in YAML syntax" — [CSR RL06.3 notes](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2-ReleaseNotes_CSR_RL06.3.txt)
- A GRCOF2 record is `GRCOF2 L M Clm Slm sigmaC sigmaS t_start(yyyymmdd.hhmm) t_end(yyyymmdd.hhmm)`. Example from TN-13: `GRCOF2    1   1 -1.979353745e-10 +3.364259942e-10 +4.5296e-11 +5.0724e-11 20260701.0000 20260801.0000` — [TN-13 CSR](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/TN-13_GEOC_CSR_RL0603.txt)
- Normalization: fully normalized with N_lm² = (2−δ0m)(2l+1)(l−m)!/(l+m)!, giving a 4π mean-square integral. Constants (µ, a_e) are in the header. "User standard deviation = scale_factor * file standard deviation". GAC/GAD include degrees 0 and 1, but these were not used in L2 processing — [L2 Handbook](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2_UserHandbook.pdf); [CSR notes](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L2-ReleaseNotes_CSR_RL06.3.txt)
- The PO.DAAC L2 granules have no file extension, e.g. `GSM-2_2026213-2026243_GRFO_UTCSR_BA01_0603` — [CMR](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GRACEFO_L2_CSR_MONTHLY_0063&provider=POCLOUD&sort_key=-start_date&page_size=2)

**TN-14 (fixed-width text)**
- Fortran format `(F7.1,F10.4,E22.13,2F8.4,E22.13,2F8.4,F8.1,F10.4)`, with columns MJD_start, yearfrac_start, C20, ΔC20 (1e-10), σC20 (1e-10), C30, ΔC30, σC30, MJD_end, yearfrac_end. "NaN" marks missing C30. Mean C20 is −4.841694573200E-04, C20 is zero tide, and GM = 0.3986004415E+06 km³/s², R = 6378.1363 km — [TN-14](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/TN-14_C30_C20_GSFC_SLR.txt)

**JPL mascon NetCDF (RL06.3Mv04 CRI)**
- Dimensions: time 258, lat 360, lon 720, i.e. 0.5° with centres at −89.75…89.75 and 0.25…359.75 east. Variables (all f8): `lwe_thickness` (cm, "Liquid_Water_Equivalent_Thickness"), `uncertainty` (cm), `land_mask` (binary), `scale_factor` ("Scale_Factor_CRI", dimensionless), `mascon_ID` (1–4551), `GAD` (cm, ocean/atmosphere dealias model), `lat_bounds`, `lon_bounds`, `time` ("days since 2002-01-01T00:00:00Z") and `time_bounds`. In this CF-1.6/ACDD-1.3 file, `land_mask`, `scale_factor` and `mascon_ID` are stored as time × lat × lon arrays. Global attributes include `time_mean_removed: 2004.000 to 2009.999`, `months_missing` (list), `GIA_removed: ICE6G-D`, `C_20_substitution`/`C_30_substitution: TN-14` and the GAD add-back note — [virtual-zarr refs](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-public/virtual_collections/TELLUS_GRAC-GRFO_MASCON_CRI_GRID_RL06.3_V4/TELLUS_GRAC-GRFO_MASCON_CRI_GRID_RL06.3_V4_virtual_https.json)

**GRACE Tellus 1° Level-3 grids (GRD-3)**
- Each grid comes with a public YAML-like `.txt` description. Dimensions are lon 360, lat 180, time 1. Global attributes include `product_version_Level3: LND_sw06_k20_G300_GCSAL_v04`, `filter_type: Gauss 300 km`, `time_mean_removed: 2005-01-01 to 2010-12-31`, destriping (Swenson & Wahr 2006), the ellipsoidal correction, `GIA_removed: ICE-6G_D`, the geocenter correction (Sun 2016), C20/C30 substitution and `license: https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-and-software/data-and-information-policy` — [GRD-3 Aug 2026 .txt](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-public/TELLUS_GRFO_L3_JPL_RL06.3_LND_v04/GRD-3_2026213-2026243_GRFO_JPLEM_BA01_0603_LND_v04.txt)

**L1B**
- Files are named `PRDID_YYYY-MM-DD_S_VV.txt`, with a YAML header terminated by `# End of YAML header`. The handbook warns not to design processing that depends on header contents — [GRACE-FO L1 Handbook](https://archive.podaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/podaac-ops-cumulus-docs/gracefo/open/docs/GRACE-FO_L1_Handbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- A robust `.gfc` parser should key on `end_of_head`, treat any unrecognised header line as a comment, accept unknown `errors` values, and infer column count from the key and the `errors`/`format` values. It should handle both E and e exponent markers and leading '+' signs, because COST-G writes `+0.10000000000E+01`.
- GRCOF2 and gfc are near-isomorphic, apart from the per-record epochs in GRCOF2 and the YAML header, so one internal coefficient-set type can serve both.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain an actual RL06.3 GSM YAML header sample: protected PO.DAAC files need EDL, and ISDC was unreachable. The exact YAML key names (e.g. `header: global_attributes:`, `earth_gravity_param`, `mean_equator_radius`, `permanent_tide_flag`) are therefore unverified here; check the "GRACE Gravity Field Solution Data Formats Document (327-732)" cited by the L2 handbook.
- The CSR mascon NetCDF variable names were not inspected (113 MB download skipped).
- The GSFC HDF5 dataset paths were not extracted from the format PDF.

## Licensing, terms of use and citation requirements

### Takeaway
NASA PO.DAAC data carry no use constraints under NASA's open data policy; cite the dataset DOI. GFZ/ICGEM/COST-G/AOD1B data are generally CC BY 4.0, cited by DOI. ESA GOCE and Swarm data are free under ESA's EO data terms; registration is needed for GOCE, and publications must credit ESA. Product-specific paper citations are expected for mascons (Watkins 2015 / Wiese 2016, Save 2016, Loomis 2019) and for ITSG (Kvas 2019).

## Future missions and expected data availability

### Takeaway
GRACE-C (NASA/DLR) is targeted for December 2028 and will use laser ranging only. NGGM (ESA) is targeted around 2032 in an inclined orbit. Together they form MAGIC. There is no published open-data statement specific to these missions yet, but GRACE-C continues the open GRACE/GRACE-FO SDS model (JPL/CSR/GFZ through PO.DAAC and ISDC).

### Cited Findings
- GRACE-C launch is targeted for Dec 2028. The fuel and orbit-decay outlook supports a GRACE-FO/GRACE-C overlap, and GFZ funds GRACE-FO operations to Dec 2029 to allow one year of cross-calibration — [Landerer 2025](https://cpaess.ucar.edu/sites/default/files/grace-fo-2025-presentation/landerer-felix.pdf); [GFZ S-NGGM page](https://www.gfz.de/en/section/global-geomonitoring-and-gravity-field/projects/s-nggm-studies-for-next-generation-gravity-missions)
- On GRACE-C, the laser interferometer will be the exclusive means of satellite-to-satellite tracking, and flight-spare GRACE-FO accelerometers are to be delivered to GRACE-C in mid-2026 — [GFZ GRACE-C page (search summary)](https://www.gfz.de/en/section/global-geomonitoring-and-gravity-field/projects/grace-c-gravity-recovery-and-climate-experiment-continuity)
- NGGM: industry status at LPS25 says it is on track for a 2032 launch in parallel with GRACE-C — [ESA LPS25 NGGM/MAGIC industry status](https://lps25.esa.int/lps25-presentations/presentations/2516/_2516.pdf). A 2026 ESA job posting describes preparing phase B2 as a FutureEO-1 mission of opportunity — [ESA jobs (search summary)](https://jobs.esa.int/job/Noordwijk-NGGM-Project-Manager/1379962833). ONERA has a €27.3M contract for the MicroSTAR accelerometers, with 7 flight models in 2028 — [ONERA press release 2024-10-22](https://onera.fr/sites/default/files/communiques/pdf/2024-10/20241022-CP-ONERA-NGGM-VA-MRB.pdf). The prime is an Airbus-led consortium — [Science in Poland](https://scienceinpoland.pl/en/news/news%2C104539%2Csener-polska-joins-important-esa-mission.html)
- MAGIC pairs GRACE-C's polar pair with NGGM's inclined pair — [Springer J Geod 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00190-024-01931-5)
- GRACE-FO RL07 was planned for "Spring '26" and GRACE RL07 for "Dec '25" — [Landerer 2025](https://cpaess.ucar.edu/sites/default/files/grace-fo-2025-presentation/landerer-felix.pdf). GFZ RL07 brings >30% noise reduction versus RL06 — [EGU26-6384](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-6384.html)
- Quantum: the NASA quantum gravity gradiometer remains a concept or pathfinder — [NTRS 20260000619](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20260000619)

### Inferences
- An app should plan for a GRACE-FO RL07 collection (likely `GRACEFO_L2_*_MONTHLY_0070`, by analogy with the GRACE RL07 naming) appearing in CMR in late 2026 or 2027. It should also plan for GRACE-C data under the same SDS file conventions after about 2029, given the stated continuity of SDS roles. NGGM/MAGIC data access will likely run through ESA, which means EO Sign In.

### Gaps
- I found no official open-data policy statements for GRACE-C, NGGM or MAGIC.
- I found no confirmation of 2026 schedule changes for GRACE-C or NGGM; the latest dated sources are from 2025.

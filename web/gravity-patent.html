Gravity Patent Rig

A 0.35 g polyethylene ball between two open high-voltage coils in a 10 cm vacuum tank, claimed to move by a gravitational field. The field, every force and torque on the ball, and its motion are computed live with the equations of the Rust crates.

The patent: about 50 kV at 10 µA. Zhang's book: 100 kV.

What is modelled, and what is not
  • Field: the coils as 24 charged rings, the four leads as wire segments, solved for their charges (gravitics::electrodes). The coils are drawn as helices.
  • Forces: weight, buoyancy, the polarisation pull, the Coulomb force, the force on the charge patches, the image in the wall, gas drag at any Knudsen number, ion drag, the Lorentz force and diamagnetism in the coil's and Earth's fields (WMM2025 at Zhang's lab), and the gravity of the field's energy.
  • Torques: the thread's torsion and damping (Q = 20), the gas's spin drag, the field on the ball's shape and on its patches, and Zhang's vortex when chosen.
  • Gas: past Paschen breakdown the gas clamps the voltage across the tank and the walls hold the rest; a glow drains the ball to its floating potential.
  • Assumed: the tank's 22 cm length and north-pointing axis, the lead layout, the supply's 50 ms rise and 1 s decay, 1% ripple, a 3 eV glow.
  • Left out: the silicone and glass dielectrics, the thread's own charge, the ripple's own forces (about 2%), radiometric forces and vibration.

What would settle it

  1. Pump below 0.1 Pa and record it.
  2. Measure the ball's charge with a Faraday cup, then neutralise it: a neutral, round ball barely moves.
  3. Swap in the PTFE ball: classical forces move it less; a gravitational field moves both alike.
  4. Swap which coil is grounded, or drive ±V/2: the live-side pull changes; a field from accelerating charges should not care.

The simulation is physica/examples/gravity_patent.rs, ported line for line; the physics is in gravitics::electrodes, gravitics::electrogravity, plasmoid::discharge and magnetics.

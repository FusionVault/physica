Gravity Patent Rig

A 0.35 g polyethylene ball between two open high-voltage coils in a 10 cm vacuum tank, claimed to move by a gravitational field. The field lines and every number are computed live from the rig's geometry.

The patent: about 50 kV at 10 µA. Zhang's book: 100 kV.
The patent only says "near vacuum". A rotary pump gives 1–100 Pa.
Sets the cotton thread's torsion constant, κ = 4π²I/T², for the polyethylene ball.
The share of the torque ceiling that the staggered ends, the helix or a seam turn into an aligning torque. Not measured: set it.

What would settle it

  1. Pump below 0.1 Pa and record it: no glow, no ions.
  2. Switch to the symmetric drive. The pull toward the live coil vanishes; a field made by the charges should not change.
  3. Swap in the PTFE ball. Classical forces give it 2.6× less acceleration; gravity gives the same.
  4. Measure the ball's charge with a Faraday cup, and time its free twist.
